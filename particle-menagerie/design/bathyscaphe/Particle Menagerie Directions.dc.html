interaction model

  • At rest: the underlined summon field and the tiny count. That is the entire product.
  • On click-select: the panel condenses beside the creature; its name surfaces in-world above it. No dock, no fixed edge — the UI lives where the creature is.
  • Scene controls: press-and-hold on empty water opens the same style of panel for light, fog, weather. Presets are the first row of that panel.
  • Ambient: everything dissolves; any keystroke re-condenses the input first, panels only if a creature was selected.

micro-interactions

  • Panel entry: hairlines and text assemble from drifting dots (~120ms gather) — no slide, no scale. Exit is the reverse.
  • Slider handle is a glowing dot that leaves a 300ms fading trail as it moves; value counts up in accent.
  • Enter: the underline ripples once outward from center; the typed word breaks into dots that swim to the formation point.

interaction model

  • At rest: a 48px hairline rail (scene light ◐, weather ≋, camera ⊙, capture ⭘, count) plus the input. Four glyphs is the whole surface.
  • Hover/click a stop: one flyout column slides from the rail. Only one flyout ever exists.
  • On select: a small instrument card docks near the creature, same styling as the rail. Presets read as choosing weather — word + three-dot forecast.
  • Ambient: the rail slides off the left edge; input fades; a lone hairline remains for 2s as a memory, then goes.

micro-interactions

  • Flyout slides 8px + fades in 240ms, cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1); never bounces.
  • Sliders move in 1% detents; the numeric readout counts through intermediate values like a meter settling.
  • A hovered rail stop brightens slowly (≈2s ease) — the glow of a passing lure, not a button highlight.

interaction model

  • Everything is language. "jellyfish" summons; "jellyfish glow 80", "weather moonlit", "release eel" adjust. Ghost autocomplete teaches the vocabulary as you type.
  • On select: one row of scrubbable word-values appears above the line — drag a number sideways to change it. No panel exists anywhere.
  • Presets are just words: "weather abyss". "who" lists the menagerie; "capture" saves a frame.
  • Ambient: even the line goes; a caret breathes at 2.4s — the only proof the water is listening.

micro-interactions

  • Enter: the typed word itself breaks into dots and swims to the formation point — the text becomes the creature.
  • Scrubbing a number emits a faint tick-dot per unit; release lets the value ease to rest.
  • Ghost completion sits after the caret at 30% white; Tab accepts it silently.

type + color

Space Mono only · 12px · ls .08em · one size, one face
white 22% ghost · 50% ambient · 90% live
#dff1ff caret only
No accent at all — hierarchy is opacity. The creatures are the only color in the world, which is the point.

what i would cut

  • Color swatches ("school of fish color rose"), every icon, every slider, the visible count.
  • The advanced section — advanced IS typing more words.
  • Honest risk: discoverability. Mitigated by ghost hints, but this direction trades first-run ease for total calm.

interaction model

  • At rest: input with its amber port light, plus the weather rotary — presets are literally a dial you turn through moods.
  • On select: a gauge plate rises near the creature and the two console dials (glow, tempo) wake to mirror it. Turn a dial or drag the plate's needle — same value.
  • "log ▸" latch holds snapshot / record / share; count is engraved inside it.
  • Ambient: needles keep drifting with the water for ~3s, then the whole console sinks out of frame.

micro-interactions

  • Dials turn with inertia and soft felt detents; the needle overshoots one degree and settles.
  • Panels surface like rising through water: blur 8px→0 + 6px upward drift over 400ms.
  • Enter: a single sonar ring expands from the port light; the ripple's radius matches the new creature's size.

type + color

Spectral small-caps 10–12px labels · IBM Plex Mono readouts

brass hairlines 18–28%
Why replace #8fd8ff entirely: amber is the maximum hue distance from the cyan creatures — chrome stays legible when a bright form passes behind it, and warm-vs-cold says "vessel vs water".

what i would cut

  • Behavior modes collapse to one 5-notch rotary inside "more".
  • No advanced section at all in v1 — twinkle/iridescence/density wait until the console earns them.
  • No color swatches; hue rides the weather ring.
